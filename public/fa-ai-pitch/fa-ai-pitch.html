The finance bottleneck

Mid-market and smaller companies often grow faster than their finance operating model. The result is manual work that quietly absorbs expensive team capacity every month.

Close depends on spreadsheets

Trackers, reconciliations, variance notes, and support files are updated manually across teams.

Reporting arrives late

FP&A spends days assembling data instead of explaining what changed and what to do next.

Approvals stall

Invoices, accrual inputs, and evidence requests get stuck in inboxes and chat threads.

Audit support is recreated

Evidence lives across ERP exports, emails, shared drives, tickets, and spreadsheets.

Exceptions are late

Duplicate invoices, unusual entries, and missing documentation are often found after review cycles begin.

Leadership waits

CFOs and operators wait for answers because trusted data is trapped inside manual pipelines.
